We recently posted our list of Compatible Wacom Pens for current or past Tablet and Cintiqs, because matching all the Wacom Pens to their respective Tablets can get confusing, especially when trying to find a replacement. This is great if you want to search by the Pen Sku or Model, but check out our new list organized by Tablets/Cintiqs instead!

Wacom devices are great, and most of them can last you a long time, unfortunately the pens usually go out before the tablet itself. Hopefully this list will help all those looking for replacement pens, especially if they are not manufactured anymore.

A general rule is that the Pens are forwards compatible, but not backwards. Most current Pro Pens are cross compatible within their respective generation. And the smaller Intuos (non pro) tablets have specific pens that cannot be used with different tablets or Cintiqs.

Check out our breakdown of most Wacom Tablets and Cintiqs, loosely organized by type and age.

NOTE:
Pens with an 'E' after the SKU denote that it has an Eraser.
If the Cintiq/Tablet SKU prefix contains an 'H' (ie- DTH), it is Touch.
If an 'L' or 'K' is present (ie- CTL or DTK), then it's NonTouch.
Tablets end their SKU with the color (if applicable), ie- K or K0=Black, B=Blue, E0=Pistachio, etc.
